ABOUT US

Driven By Passion. Obsessed With Perfection.

Hello and welcome! I am Chelaka Botheju, the founder and lead designer behind CREATIVIZR. With over 8 years of dedicated hands-on experience, I have partnered with startups, authors, musicians, and international brands to transform abstract ideas into striking, iconic visual assets.

My expertise spans the entire design workflow—from precision vector mathematics in Illustrator and CorelDRAW, to complex photo manipulation in Photoshop, editorial publishing in InDesign, and cutting-edge digital experiences in Figma and Flutter.

"At CREATIVIZR, our core philosophy is simple: To the Maximum of Creativity. We believe every brand deserves a unique identity that commands respect, builds trust, and drives conversions."
